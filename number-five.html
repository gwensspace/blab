Yo-Yos and Awakeups

Posted on August 24th, 2026



"..'Wake up' and 'awakenings' mashup brought to you by.." lol

I brought all my weights with me when I left home and I have recently started lifting... AGAIN, so, of course, my relationship with my body, fitness, discipline, the patterns shown every time I have stopped throughout my life, and what my life would have looked like had I recognized those patterns sooner- along with the correlation between them and spiritual awakenings, and societal awakenings- all have been heavy on my *chk* noggin. (If you don't know the reference, Youtube Bailey Sarian. You're welcome)

Come on, kids! Let's go on the magic school bus for a trip to memory lane and go pattern hunting.
Get comfy.


Actually, first of all, I would like to give a little disclaimer type of thing before taking you on this trip. I understand this type of topic is usually... delicate, maybe? for us, so I would like to point out the purpose of this is just to allow you in and share with you some of the craziness I've experienced/witnessed throughout my life, the subconscious programming I had and took me forever to rewire or even notice thus far. During my underage years... well, there is nothing I nor anyone can do about all that AND there's no resentment, either. It just is what it is. You know? Shit happens. I have done so much work to just let that be what it is. Any comments I make regarding my mother, trust me when I say, I am making them while giggling because I crack myself up. Do I wish to break the No Contact? Absolutely not, lmao. I do wish she gets to experience healing and peace before she leaves this realm, though. And as an adult, which I sort of consider since leaving home and getting clean at 20, (I mean 18 and 19 were kind of a blackout, so am I wrong?) I just did the best I could with what I had at the time, every time, and I cap this little "hunt the patterns" quest at December 2024, just because since getting to Mexico, 1- the shock and adaptation process of leaving everything I know and all my loved ones for this shitshow I've been living has forced me to put all my focus on my mental health, nervous system regulation and just staying alive, so I have been refusing to be anything but gentle with myself when it comes to my body. and 2- I have recently found out via a doc's visit, blood work and ultrasounds, there's a growing fibroid in my uterus that needs to be removed (uterus and all- yay), in addition to, apparently, adrenal fatigue- which I've been working on with supplements and a somewhat strict sleep schedule. And then if you take into consideration the fact that I'm like in my late 30s (what a fucking surprise and miracle lmao) so hormone changes, metabolism changes, blahblahblah. So, the past year and almost nine months just makes the data kind of irrelevant for what this entry talks about. You know? I don't know. Let me know.

The interesting thing is I have always enjoyed moving my body, being active, eating healthy, eating clean. I remember as a kid-kid, being on a trip with family, staying in a hotel room, and doing as many pushups as I could, just because, and on another flashback, crunches. Not sure if it was something I saw on TV or a family member doing- who knows. I always enjoyed how it felt- how I felt. Then, I remember doing some swimming and competing and then being taken out of it. The same with soccer, kickball, and whatever else. I don't remember ever there being any consistency with any of the things that kids do to learn about disciplicine and teamwork. I want to say I asked my mother about that once (as an adult) and her response was something along the lines of "because you asked to be removed from it", which makes no sense, either, because how many kids don't want to do things they normally want to do at some point..right? Also, on the other hand, remembering her spending money on and giving cash to her deadbeat boyfriend flavor of the year each year made more sense.. but hey, whatevs. In Junior High School, I tried to join a soccer team, but apparently I couldn't join because I didn't have health insurance. And now that I think about it, this was around the time my visa stopped getting renewed, so it more than likely played a role in that. Who knows.

At 20, after getting clean, I intuitively started doing home bodyweight workouts first thing from waking up every day. When I started staying in/moved to Montgomery, AL, I would run around the neighborhoods I was staying in, join friends at their gyms, do some high repped lifting, low carb/low fat nutrition for the most part, because I still love eating, desserts and such. It was all about the look, though. I tried to make that as consistent as I could even though I kept having to move from here to there, funds were never really great, immigration scams, instability everywhere. You know the dance if you've read these entries in order, lol. When I moved to Tennessee in 2016, I got introduced to Powerlifting and I fell in LOVE with it. I fell in love with being strong and being able to eat like a male powerlifter (lmao. due to lack of information, btw. Now I now better, so if I'm ever in a position to train again, I'd know what not to do haha), so at that point, the "look" stopped being important and it all became about strength- I was even training to compete. But of course, that was the year the whole DACA renewal got delayed because, you know, the U.S. goververnment loves to do whatever tf it wants, WHEN it wants, so, THAT + couch hopping, once again + the job loss + being a few months away from graduating massage therapy school.. I just couldn't focus enough to just.. anything but try to make it to graduation- oh and forget about gym fees and the food I needed to train. The sudden fear of not being able to train and lift successfully without the amount of food I thought I needed was so intense, I just literally hard stopped. In my head, it made no sense how it would even work. Over the years, I have seen many athletes share war stories about being homeless and eating a slice of bread and hitting PR, but even if I would have run across those types of people and stories, I wouldn't have been able to do anything because of the way it felt in my body. That intense feeling of empty, weak, shaky, cold type of paralizing whatever or panic.. I don't even know. Honestly, I have no clue how tf I made it without doing drugs. LMAO! Oh yea. Wait. Yeah... I smoked a lot of American Spirits and did a few sweat lodges hahaha.

I believe I was about 50lbs overweight by that point. When I was lifting, that weight didn't matter, it had a purpose and not all of it was adipose tissue, but once I stopped training and got into this fight or flight sedentary phase for the next few months, it stopped feeling fine. After graduation, I moved back to Virginia because I felt like something kept pulling me back "home" and I thought Virginia Beach was the closest that felt like home, at the time. After a year and things not falling into place, (mostly because I kept being hard headed enough to continue trying to do sales over and over again because I realized massage therapy as a career wasn't my thing. Newsflash- I am great at a lot of things, I am a fast learner, AND also I'm a "back of the house" type of worker- not front of the house. With that said, I don't suck more at anything than sales. I am just not good at it. AT ALL) it felt like that "needing to go back home" feeling I kept getting was back home-home in Alabama, so I went. Everything started falling into place back where I grew up and graduated high school from (War Eagle!), the house with the fenced in yard for Mr King Koopa, the job, the mother's attitude (lol), the bills. The setup was perfect... until it wasn't. The majority of that year, I got the stability I was looking for, my body and nervous system started to feel settled and regulated. I believe that year's purpose was to show me that yes, I have a part in things, but so does the other person involved. "What I allow, continues" and from the year I got clean and left home until then, I was able to witness my life with and without disrespect, abuse, and anything in between, and I just didn't (and still don't) see my life being miserable and accepting whatever treatment from anyone, anymore- regardless of their title in my life. I stayed in Alabama- just on the other end, for a few more years because I wanted more of that stability juice, I was so ready to stop moving around like that. I figured staying in the same region would be a good break. I ended up getting in a cute, little, tiny studio apartment in Daphne, after some more roommate drama. The neighborhood was quiet and clean, and Koopa and I could walk for miles every day. Those were the most stable years I've ever had in my entire life! Psht- Even the job at the time was less than a five minute drive from home before going full remote, so lunch breaks were spent walking the Koop. Stability, routine, accessibility to outdoors (comfortably) got me to the best shape I have ever been. First time I felt safe in my body- in my skin, loving how my body moved, especially how my clothes fit, haha.

Nutrition was another thing. Although I'm a "foodie" and love anything and everything that tastes good and has awesome texture, my body has always craved healthy, clean, and consistent type of foods for most of the time. That's why the experience with powerlifting and meal prepping the same things every week or two just worked so well, because once I got done cooking that one day, I didn't have to think at all about "what to eat" for the next couple of weeks- I'd just grab, warmup, and eat. The routine was everything. It still is- just not at that level of quantity, lol, but yes. ---Until it's time to self-soothe due to some new major event happening, of course.

Everything was almost perfect and I finally felt what people describe feeling in their first year in recovery- you know, "omg, I put the substances down, got a job, got a house, started back school, got a car, got the girl, proposed, life is great.." (barf. lmao) It took me ten years to even get to this point, it was a beautiful thing, though. I was so grateful. Then, I felt it was time to try to climb the ladder because even though I had gotten a couple of promotions, I still was living paycheck to paycheck and that never sat well with me, haha. I got help paying the course and got my independent insurance adjuster license. Let me tell you what- In the first three days of just training, I made what normally would have taken me a whole month and a half to make. When I tell you I had never jumped ship so quick... Ooof. The schedule was intense and insane- yes. No more lunch break walks with the koop- unfortunately. but 1- remote work was in the horizon, and 2- I felt like Monica Geller and I loved it. The budget was budgetting, the bills that could be paid ahead of time were paid months in advance, the credit cards were always paid up, the savings account had more money than the checking one. And then because I still had left over, I got to allow myself to gift myself what I wanted growing up- I wanted video game consoles as a kid so I started with gifting me an Xbox, and other things I wanted and never got- boom- got it, tipping stopped being a concern, so hey- just tip what the bill is- buttfuck it. I hate heights, but let's go skydiving-multiple times- YOLO. It was the best time EVER. haha definitely good times. It did my nervous system good. 10/10 recommend.

Then I got COVID at the end of September, 2021. For whatever reason, that shitshow showed me so much about my body. Although, I was in the best shape of my life and had never felt better in my meat suit, about the month prior to getting COVID, I was feeling angsty all throughout. I couldn't figure it out. Something just felt off and like something bad was going to happen, so I kept noticing how the very first thing I wanted to do was eat whatever just to self-soothe. I didn't act on it most of the time, it was just nice to notice it, but I still couldn't figure out the reason for it. Then a month later, I get COVID. I couldn't smell anything at all for months, as if someone had just unplugged my nose and life just smelled like a black and white tv show- if that even makes sense. Oh and things I ate the most tasted rotten for the first couple of years, even though they weren't. I became SO paranoid I would end up burning the apartment down with Koopa in it when cooking and I couldn't deal. I had to get a rice cooker for the first time ever because I could always smell the rice being done, but I couldn't do that anymore. I became paranoid there was going to be a gas leak and I wasn't going to be able to smell it and that's how Koopa and I were going to go, haha. I became insanely hyperaware of my inability to smell, even for the simplest things... I don't know, I just could-not-turn-my-mind-off. I should've sought therapy, well I did, but that's a whole entire drama for another entry. I love my current therapist, though- shout out. lmao I think our two year anniversary is coming up. ANYWAY, for veggies, I relied on canned green beans, which worked out because-one less thing to worry about. For protein, I couldn't eat white meat at all- it tasted rotten no matter what, red meat was more bearable, and spicy pistachios to be able to at least feel the food from my mouth and down to its destination.

Well, that's what I'd eat in public-aka work, anyway. (Which honestly, was an absolute pain in the ass because on one hand, I liked eating at my desk so I could eat and work, but the damn women coworkers were always commenting on how they wished they could meal prep, but could never because their kids would eat their food, their kids this, and their kids that, and this food, and that food.. fack! all while acting as if I was the worst supervisor ever. First of all, if my job is literally reviewing and approving your file-EACH file before you're able to move forward with it/them and close them, why would you insist on keeping me from reviewing the thing to begin with? Then any suggestions I make on the things you send to me are dumb so I get tattletaled on dumber shit- as if I'm going to get in trouble. Eavesdropping on conversations and then spreading around shit that was allegedly said, but untrue. As if that damn structure of the industry wasn't stressful enough. Oh, and especially when they switched MY supervisor to the most condescending ass, dismissive ass, close minded ass, two faced ass asshole ever. Like Fuck all the way right off and to the left. Maybe they were trying to give me mal de ojo and that's how we got here. LMAO. Anyway, that's my soapbox about that. I'm glad I was able to finally get it out after all this time, thanks for letting me share ♥)
At home, however, that Chocolate Trinity Ice Cream from Publix was my bff. It's chocolate ice cream with chocolate chunks, chocolate swirls, and chocolate fudge thingies. I couldn't taste it at that point, but the texture along with the temperature was sooooooo soothing... But that's all I ate at home and spicy popcorn, so... definitely not good, lmao.

Well, all that brings us to the patterns situation deal. All the feelings from that month before to all those adjustments in nutrition and cooking ways after- the way I spiralled, all brought me so many memories of growing up. All those times I remember being active, I remember my body feeling great and whatnot, but the times I'd get pulled out and would be sedentary, I remember junk food being more accessible than any other times and my body just blowing up-I'd gain weight so quick, it felt like. Every time. Activity levels fluctuating, my body weight fluctuating. Additionally, for whatever reason, all the fluctuation coincided with the times something inappropriate would happen with an older male, and during one of the blowup phases, one of my mother's boyfriends at the time said to me and in front her that my back looked like "cows' titties." I didn't get to learn what someone standing up for me felt like until after I got clean, but that's neither here nor there on here. That was the pattern growing up. And as an adult, though, it's been more like: the angst, then activity levels (which have been able to coincide with the changes around me), my body weight fluctuating, getting sick and tired of being sick and tired, readjusting, wash/rinse/repeat.

Because I had more money than I ever had at this point, I felt the next step was to own a house, so I bought one. I originally wanted a tiny house or an RV, because I knew I didn't want to be in Alabama forever. I wanted to travel a lot and all the time, but I was SO exhausted of moving around and not being able to sleep in my own bed, in my space, so I wanted some sort of stability and consistency- not just for me, but for the Koop, too. However, with all that spiraling, I just could-not-stay-still and I just needed to make it go away and do something, so, I bought the three bedroom, 2 bathroom house with the yard in Mobile, AL for valentine's day. Talk about trying to ease some anxiety.. LMAO. That was the worst thing I could have done at that time (and place.) HAHA. I figured "welp. I will just rent it out to a family, eventually, for a reasonable price- I won't be a regular landlord, I'll be a cool landlord. OR just turn it into a recovery house. It'll be great." It wasn't. It didn't turnout that way at all. I sold that lemon asap and lost money on it. But this isn't about the house nor my poor decisions. lmao. Focus

Okay, so, from the COVID fiasco, I gained about 40 lbs in less than 3 months. Even though appetite wasn't the best because I couldn't smell and things tasted weird, weight wasn't going anywhere, muscle mass was gone. Energy levels were absolutely stupid. I couldn't even lift an empty barbell. A barbell usually weights 35-45lbs- depending on the gym. 20 lbs dumbbells? forget about it. Regular, simple bodyweight workouts? psht, they made me sleep for 4-6 hours every time. I felt worthless af. I even did BJJ for several months before bed, because why not. The first time I was able to make it through a workout AND not feel fatigued afterwards was in the spring of 2024 AND it was crossfit- shoutout. LMAO. And guess what! That summer, I was finally back in Albuquerque for good, in our RV, Sophie, in the perfect spot with a big ass yard for the Mister King Koopa Alexander Cornholio The Great Madrid, the routine was starting to routine, I was finally figuring out the 1099-contractor life in my field, I met my therapist, life was great.. Annnnnnnnd orange diaper rash gets elected and here Koopa and I are in Mexico.

Have you been able to spot the patterns, yet? lol

You know, in the recovery community, the talk about spiritual awakenings gets brought up all the time, and for someone who takes things literally, I really thought that was it- that's where it all ended- that was the end goal... Don't skew my perception of reality, maintain active inventories on myself, help others- all constant, never ending things- and things fall into place. And that's it! That made sense. I didn't start with the ability to see the world outside of my hoola hoop- it gradually, slooowwwwly happened, a tiny bit at a time, throughout the years. By that happening, the ability to love and accept myself a little more each time started to even be a thing. By those happening, more and more of societal awakenings kept happening. And THEN by those happening, have I only been able to genuinely feel others' love toward me. Sort of like a zipper slowly zipping up. And like good ol' Miranda Bailey and many others in real life would say "If you're not angry, you're not paying attention." And guess what! It's even worse and getting worse in Latin America the rest of the world every second.
I mean, name one country that isn't going through injustices against BIPOC, women, and kids.

So, the patterns? Just like any other living organism, consistency, stability, having basic needs met, all are my nervous system's bff. And learning to regulate my nervous system (and attempt to keep it that way most of the time) during shitshow inhumane chaos has been an adventure, to say the least. It definitely has not been possible to even attempt without the people in my life. And for that I'm forever grateful.

Thank you for being here and for reading,
