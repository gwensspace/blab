Outer Citizen

Posted on June 16, 2025/Edited August 11th, 2026




I’ve never really felt like I belonged to just one place. Born in Venezuela, raised in the United States, now navigating life in Mexico — I’ve met people, lost people, gained languages, and even regained lost ones (lol). I carry stories across borders and time zones, a patchwork of every version of me I’ve been.
Being an Outer Citizen isn’t about distance — it’s about perspective. I’ve always existed between worlds: cultures, careers, even dimensions. But through it all, I’ve come to realize that we are all connected — not by borders or systems, but by energy, experience, and existence itself.
Maybe we’re all Outer Citizens in some way, orbiting the same truth: that we belong not just somewhere… but everywhere.
..Regardless of "immigration status" 😘


Look. Honestly, I've always been insecure about writing. I don't consider myself a writer, a content creator, an artist, a creative.. nothing really. I just exist and occupy space. So, when I took the first programming course and made this site, my intention was to just blog about dumb shit, market my new skill and stores, and hopefully be able to make some income. Clearly, if you haven't been able to tell, I have edited the shit out this site, taken everything off and left it as just a place where I can just document my life and make sure it'll stay up for as long as github is around and even after I pass.

With all that said, that entry above was edited by AI that June 16, 2025, since I didn't think my own words were good enough. Fast forward to today and the intention I have with this blogging thing, I just simply don't give a fuck if my writing isn't good enough. Especially since the longer I'm away from the U.S. and the English language, the more I feel like I am forgetting all that grammar I once felt so self-righteous about. lol.

This is what I sound like, this how I communicate. Every time I have read the above, I don't recognize it as my own. I mean, I relate with it, but those aren't my words, and I'm not completely deleting it for transparency purposes.

So, here is me, here is why I have created this, and if you are a regular visitor/reader, you may notice the constant changes to the layout and the coding, because my intention is to continue taking in as many free online programming/coding courses I can get my hands on and be able to just create things on the fly without the need of templates, the internet, etc.- ESPECIALLY without AI since it's killing the planet.

I love how this experience creating this site and adjusting it reminds me of the time Myspace was a thing and we could edit the layouts and add all sorts of things to the code.


Anyway, thank you for being here and reading,
